# Prompt for writing a quick post with Claude or ChatGPT

Copy everything between the two lines below into Claude or ChatGPT. Replace the topic on the first
line. Then copy each part of the answer into the matching box in **Quick posts (agents)**.

---

Write a blog post for kirbychanmarkham.com, the Markham, Ontario website of Kirby Chan & Co. Real
Estate Team, eXp Realty Brokerage.

Topic: [WRITE YOUR TOPIC HERE, for example "home inspections for Markham buyers"]

Give me the answer in exactly these labelled parts, in this order, and nothing else:

HEADLINE: 40 to 70 characters. The words a Markham buyer or seller would type into Google, written
as a natural headline.

SUMMARY: one or two sentences, 120 to 160 characters, saying what the reader will learn.

CATEGORY: one of buying, selling, neighbourhoods, market, condos, new-construction,
moving-to-markham, downsizing, investing, costs-and-taxes.

QUICK ANSWER: two to four sentences that directly answer the question in the headline, so it
could stand alone as a Google answer.

POST: 800 to 1,400 words, in Markdown, inside one code block so I can copy it exactly.
- Do not repeat the headline at the top. Start with a paragraph that answers the question.
- 4 to 7 sections, each starting with "## " and a heading written as a question or phrase people
  search for. Never use a single "#".
- Short paragraphs of one to four sentences. Use a Markdown table when comparing options or costs,
  a numbered list for steps and bullets for checklists.
- Link to at least three of these pages where they genuinely fit, using descriptive link text
  and the exact paths shown: /buyers/, /sellers/, /home-valuation/, /neighbourhoods/,
  /downsizing-markham/, /first-time-home-buyers-markham/, /relocating-to-markham/,
  /upsizing-markham/, /new-construction-markham/, /investment-property-markham/,
  /luxury-homes-markham/, /selling-an-estate-home-markham/,
  /selling-a-home-after-separation-markham/, /market-reports/, /map-of-markham/, /contact/,
  and neighbourhood pages such as /unionville-markham/, /cornell-markham/,
  /markham-village-markham/, /berczy-village-markham/, /cathedraltown-markham/,
  /wismer-markham/, /greensborough-markham/, /angus-glen-markham/, /box-grove-markham/,
  /thornhill-markham/, /milliken-mills-markham/, /downtown-markham/.
- End with a short "What to do next" section and one sentence inviting the reader to
  [contact us](/contact/).

FAQ: four to six questions a reader would really ask, each ending with a question mark, each with
a one to three sentence answer. Format as "Q: ..." then "A: ..." on the next line.

SOURCES: every source you relied on for a figure, rate, date or rule, one per line as
"Name | https://full-link". Use government and regulator sites first (canada.ca, ontario.ca,
cmhc-schl.gc.ca, reco.on.ca, markham.ca, york.ca, trreb.ca). Never another brokerage's or agent's
blog. If you are not certain of a figure, leave it out rather than guess.

NEIGHBOURHOODS: any of angus-glen, berczy-village, box-grove, cathedraltown, cornell, downtown,
greensborough, markham-village, milliken-mills, thornhill, unionville, wismer that the post is
mainly about, or "none".

SERVICES: any of first-time-buyers, downsizing, upsizing, relocation, luxury, new-construction,
investors, estate-sales, separation-and-divorce that fit, or "none".

Writing rules. The website rejects the post if any of these are broken:
- Canadian spelling: neighbourhood, centre, colour, organize is fine, metre.
- No em dashes or en dashes anywhere, including ranges. Write "3 to 6 months".
- Never put a comma directly before "and" or "or". Write "A, B and C", and rewrite any sentence
  that needs one.
- No exclamation marks, no emoji, no italics.
- None of these phrases: "In today's market", "navigating", "delve", "unlock", "game changer",
  "nestled", "vibrant", "bustling", "hidden gem", "look no further", "dream home",
  "It's important to note", "when it comes to", "In conclusion", and no sentences shaped like
  "It's not about X, it's about Y".
- Write as the team ("we"), plainly and specifically. Explain jargon the first time.

Compliance rules. This is advertising by a registered Ontario brokerage:
- Never invent clients, stories, quotes, statistics or results.
- Every number, rate, date or rule must be current and come from a source you list.
- No predictions of where prices or rates will go. No "best", "top" or "guaranteed" claims.
- Do not promise a school catchment. Say boundaries must be confirmed with the school board.
- Nothing about who "should" live in a neighbourhood, and nothing about safety or crime.
- Explain how rules work. Do not give personal legal, tax or mortgage advice.
