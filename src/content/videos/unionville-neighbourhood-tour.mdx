## About this video

We walk Main Street Unionville from end to end, then head through the mature residential belt and down toward Toogood Pond, talking about what each pocket costs and who tends to buy there.

The part most buyers have not thought about is the difference between the historic core, the mature belt and the Highway 7 corridor. They share a name and they do not share a market.
