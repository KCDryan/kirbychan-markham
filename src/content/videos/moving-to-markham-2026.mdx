## About this video

Kirby Chan, Broker, of Kirby Chan & Co. Real Estate Team covers what buyers actually need to know before moving to Markham. The video looks at neighbourhood differences, home prices, schools, commute times and what daily life feels like across the city.

Areas like Unionville, Cornell, Berczy Village and Downtown Markham each offer a different lifestyle, price point and long-term value. Kirby explains how comparing them can lead to a more informed decision.

## The two deadlines that matter on arrival

**Health coverage has none.** Ontario states plainly that there is no longer a waiting period for OHIP, so you can apply as soon as you arrive rather than waiting three months. Bring three original documents: one proving citizenship or OHIP-eligible immigration status, one proving Ontario residency and one proving identity.

**Your driving licence effectively expires after 60 days.** You may drive on a valid licence from another province, state or country for 60 days after moving. After that you need an Ontario one. The part that catches people is the evidence: alongside the licence itself you need original documents proving your driving experience in English or French. Those are far easier to obtain before you leave than afterwards.

## What to know before you buy here

Markham is in York Region, so a purchase carries Ontario land transfer tax only. The same purchase inside the City of Toronto attracts a second municipal tax of comparable size, which on a median priced home runs to thousands of dollars.

Schools are assigned by home address, so the address decides the school rather than the other way around. Confirm the boundary with the school board for the specific address before committing, because boundaries change and a listing is not a reliable source.
