## About this video

Kirby Chan, Broker, of Kirby Chan & Co. Real Estate Team explains why he never pushes clients to buy or sell. He explains why clarity leads to better long-term decisions.

He talks about understanding your personal timing and evaluating market conditions without urgency, so you can move forward with confidence whether you plan to move now or are just exploring your options.
