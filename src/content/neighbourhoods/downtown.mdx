## The short answer

Downtown Markham is the one part of the city built to be walked. Condo towers, ground floor restaurants, a cinema, public squares and the York University Markham Campus, all laid out on a street grid on the Highway 7 corridor.

If you are moving from downtown Toronto and dreading the suburbs, this is usually the first place we take you.

## What homes cost

TRREB does not report Downtown Markham as its own community, so there is no neighbourhood median to quote. The table above uses Markham-wide figures instead. In August 2026 the condo apartment median across the whole City of Markham was $563,800 on 57 sales and the condo townhouse median was $750,000 on 28 sales (TRREB Market Watch). The nearest TRREB communities are Unionville and Village Green-South Unionville, but TRREB does not say which one covers this area.

Condo pricing works differently from house pricing and the difference catches people out. Two units with the same asking price can carry very different monthly costs once maintenance fees are counted and fees are not a fixed feature of a building. Read the status certificate and the reserve fund study every time, without exception.

New supply is also a factor. Buildings continue to be delivered in this area and that affects both resale competition and rents in a way it does not in a finished neighbourhood.

## The campus and what it changed

The York University Markham Campus brought students, staff and daytime activity into the centre. For residents it means a more active street level than a purely residential condo district and for investors it means a rental pool that did not exist a decade ago.

## Amenities and daily life

Restaurants, a cinema, grocery and services are at street level or a short walk away. Public squares and programmed space give the area a centre, which almost nothing else in Markham has.

The honest trade-off: it is new. The public realm is still maturing, some blocks are still construction sites and the neighbourhood does not yet have the settled feel of Unionville a few minutes away. If you want mature trees and quiet, this is the wrong address.

## Transit and the commute

Unionville GO on the Stouffville line is a short distance south and runs into Union Station. Viva bus rapid transit runs along Highway 7, connecting west toward Richmond Hill and Vaughan and east toward Cornell. Highway 404 and Highway 407 are both close.

Scheduled weekday morning trains take 41 minutes from Unionville GO to Union Station (GO Transit timetable, September 2026).

## Who Downtown Markham suits

It suits people relocating from downtown Toronto, first time buyers who want walkability, downsizers leaving a house in Markham who want to stay in the city and investors focused on transit and the campus.

It does not suit buyers who want a yard, mature streets or a detached house.
