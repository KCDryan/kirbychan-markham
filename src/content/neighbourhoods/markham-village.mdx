## The short answer

Markham Village is where the city started. Main Street Markham still functions as a real main street rather than a weekend destination, the housing stock runs from century homes to 1990s builds and the lots in the older core are often deeper than anything you will find in newer north Markham.

If Unionville is the postcard, Markham Village is the working version. You give up some of the polish and you get more land and, on recent TRREB figures, a lower detached price.

## The pockets that matter

The **historic core** sits along and around Main Street Markham North and South. Century houses, some converted to commercial use, mixed with post war infill.

The **mature belt** spreads east and north from the core. This is the bulk of the market: 1960s through 1990s detached homes and bungalows, mature trees, wide streets.

The **newer edges** toward Ninth Line and north toward Wismer bring later subdivisions and townhomes, with smaller lots and newer mechanicals.

## What homes cost

From January to March 2026 TRREB recorded 26 sales in Markham Village with a median of $1,209,000 across all property types. The table above shows the detached and condo townhouse figures for the same period. TRREB reports Old Markham Village separately: 6 sales with a median of $1,040,000 in the same quarter.

The reason to pay attention here is the rebuild premium. A tired bungalow on a deep lot in the older core is not priced as a house. It is priced as land and it competes with builders. If you are buying to live in rather than to rebuild, look slightly outside the core and the maths improves quickly.

## Schools

Markham District High School is the main YRDSB secondary school for much of the neighbourhood. Elementary options include both public boards. Boundaries are reviewed periodically and they do change, so confirm the catchment for the exact address rather than for the area.

## Parks, amenities and daily life

Main Street carries independent restaurants, a few long established shops and the civic buildings. Day to day shopping happens along Highway 7 and Markham Road. The Markham Fairgrounds sit on the north edge of the neighbourhood and bring one busy week a year with them.

Parks and the Rouge tributary trails run through the area and the older streets have the tree canopy to match their age.

The honest trade-off: older housing means older systems. Budget for it. The other trade-off is Highway 7 and Markham Road traffic, which is real and which shapes where you will and will not want to be on a corner lot.

## Transit and the commute

Markham GO sits on the Stouffville line into Union Station. YRT routes run along Highway 7 and Markham Road, connecting to Viva bus rapid transit on the Highway 7 corridor. Highway 407 access is a short drive.

Scheduled weekday morning trains take 52 minutes from Markham GO to Union Station (GO Transit timetable, September 2026).

## Thinking of selling in Markham Village?

Markham Village's housing stock spans from century homes near Main Street through 1960s to 1990s detached houses and bungalows to newer infill and townhomes, so no single figure describes the market well. A deep lot near Main Street can be priced as land rather than as a house. The median across all property types was $1,209,000 on 26 sales from January to March 2026, with the detached median close to $1,265,000 (TRREB). ## Downsizing in Markham Village

Markham Village is one of the more natural places to downsize without leaving the area, because the housing stock already includes smaller bungalows from the 1960s and 1970s alongside condo townhomes near the older core. From January to March 2026 the condo townhouse median was about $749,000 on a small sample of 4 sales, well under the detached median of about $1,265,000, so check current listings rather than relying on that figure alone.

For longtime residents the appeal is staying close to Main Street and the GO station without maintaining a large lot. A smaller bungalow keeps you in a house rather than a condo, while a condo townhome trades the yard entirely for lower upkeep. Both options sit within a short walk of Markham GO for anyone who still wants the train without driving. ## Who Markham Village suits

It suits buyers who want land and are willing to renovate. It suits downsizers who have lived in the area for decades and want to stay. It suits first time buyers who want a central location and a house rather than a condo, at lower detached prices than Unionville.

It does not suit buyers who want everything new and finished. For that, look at Cornell, Greensborough or Wismer.
