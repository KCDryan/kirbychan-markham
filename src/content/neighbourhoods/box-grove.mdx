## The short answer

Box Grove is a nineteenth century hamlet that woke up one decade to find subdivisions all around it. The result is a small, self contained pocket in Markham's southeast corner with newer housing and easy Highway 407 access.

It is the neighbourhood for people whose commute runs east or west rather than downtown.

## What homes cost

From January to March 2026 TRREB recorded 8 sales in Box Grove with a median of $1,177,500 across all property types. With so few sales, only the detached row in the table above has enough sales for TRREB to report a price.

In the same period the detached median in Box Grove, about $1,345,000 on 5 sales, sat below the detached medians TRREB reported for Berczy, Wismer and Greensborough. Distance from the GO stations and proximity to the highway are part of the reason, which means the value case is strong for drivers and weak for train commuters.

## The hamlet and what is left of it

A small cluster of older buildings survives from the original settlement. It is modest and it is worth seeing, because it is the only thing distinguishing Box Grove visually from any other 2000s subdivision.

## Parks, amenities and daily life

Parks and trail connections run through the newer sections. The Boxgrove retail area covers groceries and day to day needs. Anything larger is a drive along Markham Road or Highway 7.

The honest trade-off: Highway 407 sits right at the southern edge. On the closest streets you will hear it. Go and stand on the street at rush hour before you decide, because this is not something a listing photo will tell you.

## Transit and the commute

Highway 407 access is immediate, which is the neighbourhood's strongest practical feature. YRT local routes serve the area. Markham GO on the Stouffville line is a drive north.

Scheduled weekday morning trains take 52 minutes from Markham GO to Union Station, plus the drive to the station (GO Transit timetable, September 2026).

## Downsizing in Box Grove

Box Grove offers a narrower set of downsizing options than most Markham neighbourhoods. The property mix is mostly detached homes and freehold townhomes from the mid 2000s onward, with a small cluster of older hamlet properties, so a downsizer staying local is usually moving from a larger detached home into one of the freehold townhomes rather than a condo since condo stock here is minimal.

TRREB recorded so few sales in Box Grove from January to March 2026 that it could not report a separate freehold townhouse price, so use the detached median of about $1,345,000 on 5 sales as a starting point and confirm current townhome pricing against active listings. The Boxgrove retail area covers day to day needs and Highway 407 access suits downsizers who want a smaller property without giving up an easy drive to family elsewhere. Our [downsizing in Markham guide](/downsizing-markham/) walks through the wider process.

## Who Box Grove suits

It suits first time buyers, drivers commuting east or west on Highway 407 and anyone who wants newer detached housing without paying north Markham detached prices.

It does not suit downtown train commuters or buyers who want mature character streets.
