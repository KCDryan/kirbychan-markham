## The short answer

Berczy Village is north Markham family housing at its most straightforward. Late 1990s and 2000s detached homes and townhomes on quiet crescents, plenty of parks and a school reputation that a lot of buyers move here specifically to access.

If you are relocating with school aged children and you want a low surprise purchase, this is usually one of the first two or three neighbourhoods we show you.

One local reality worth naming plainly: the school catchment premium here is real and it is priced in. Do not let an agent tell you a boundary is guaranteed. Boards review boundaries periodically and buyers who paid a premium for a catchment can find themselves outside it.

## Schools

Pierre Elliott Trudeau High School is the secondary school most associated with the neighbourhood and it carries a strong local reputation. Elementary options run through both YRDSB and YCDSB.

Confirm the catchment for the specific address, in writing, with the board. This is the single most common mistake we see families make in north Markham.

## Parks, amenities and daily life

Berczy is built around its parks and trail connections and the newer streets have wide sidewalks and generous green space between blocks. Shopping is a drive rather than a walk, mostly to the plazas along Major Mackenzie Drive and Highway 7.

The honest trade-off: this is quiet suburban living. It is genuinely quiet, which some buyers love and others find isolating. There is no main street, no cafe culture and very little night life.

## Transit and the commute

YRT local routes serve the neighbourhood and connect south to the Highway 7 rapid transit corridor. There is no GO station in Berczy, so most downtown commuters drive to Mount Joy or Unionville and take the Stouffville line from there. Highway 404 is the usual route for drivers.

## Who Berczy Village suits

It suits families with school aged children, relocating buyers who want certainty and anyone upsizing out of a condo or a townhome closer to the city.

It does not suit buyers who want walkable amenities, a short train ride or a character home.
