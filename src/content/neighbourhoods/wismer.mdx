## The short answer

Wismer Commons is what most people mean when they say north Markham. Family housing from the early 2000s onward, quiet streets, plenty of parks and Mount Joy GO close enough that a train commute is realistic.

Wismer does not have much personality. It works and for many buyers that is exactly the point.

## What homes cost

From January to March 2026 TRREB recorded 39 sales in Wismer with a median of $1,048,000 across all property types. The table above breaks that down by property type for the same period.

Wismer and Berczy Village compete for the same buyer and their detached medians were close in the same quarter. When we are comparing two listings across that line, the deciding factors are almost always the school catchment and the drive to Mount Joy GO, not the houses themselves.

## Schools

Bur Oak Secondary School serves much of Wismer, with elementary options through both public boards. As everywhere in north Markham, confirm the catchment for the exact address in writing rather than relying on a listing description.

## Parks, amenities and daily life

Parks and trail links run through the neighbourhood and the street layout is friendly to walking and cycling within it. Shopping is a drive, mostly to plazas along Major Mackenzie Drive, Bur Oak Avenue and Markham Road.

The honest trade-off: the tree canopy is young and the architecture is repetitive. If character matters to you, Markham Village will feel very different for similar money.

## Transit and the commute

Mount Joy GO on the Stouffville line is the practical route downtown. YRT local routes connect the neighbourhood south toward the Highway 7 corridor. Highway 404 is the usual driving route.

Scheduled weekday morning trains take 57 minutes from Mount Joy GO to Union Station (GO Transit timetable, September 2026).

## Downsizing in Wismer

Wismer is mostly known as an upsizing destination, but it also has real options for anyone looking to move to a smaller property without leaving north Markham. The condo apartment median from January to March 2026 was about $478,000 against a detached median of about $1,418,000, a gap of roughly $940,000, with semis at about $1,040,000 and freehold townhomes at about $1,112,000 sitting in between as a smaller-house alternative to a condo.

Because most of the housing dates from the early 2000s onward, mechanicals are newer across every property type here, which keeps maintenance costs down for a downsizer regardless of which size they choose. Mount Joy GO within reach of the neighbourhood also means giving up a large house does not have to mean giving up an easy train commute. See our [downsizing in Markham guide](/downsizing-markham/) for how to approach the move.

## Who Wismer suits

It suits families upsizing into their long term house, relocating buyers who want a low risk purchase and train commuters who do not want to pay Unionville detached prices.

It does not suit buyers who want character, mature trees or walkable amenities.
