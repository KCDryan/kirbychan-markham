## The short answer

Cornell is Markham's planned community experiment and it mostly worked. Streets are laid out on a grid instead of the usual suburban loops, houses face the street with real front porches and the cars are pushed to lanes behind. The result is a neighbourhood that feels more like a town and less like a subdivision.

The trade is private space. Lots are small, yards are modest and you will know your neighbours whether you planned to or not.

## What homes cost

From January to March 2026 TRREB recorded 40 sales in Cornell with a median of $977,000 across all property types. The table above breaks that down by property type for the same period.

Cornell is the most transparent market in Markham. When twenty houses share a floor plan, buyers can compare directly and sellers cannot hide behind a lack of comparables. That cuts both ways: it protects buyers from overpaying and it means a seller needs genuine condition and presentation advantages to beat the pack.

## Schools, the hospital and daily life

Markham Stouffville Hospital sits on the neighbourhood's edge, which matters to medical staff and to anyone thinking about care access as they age. The Cornell Community Centre and library anchor the middle of the neighbourhood.

Elementary schools serve the area through both public boards, with secondary options in east Markham. Confirm catchment boundaries for a specific address rather than for the neighbourhood, because they are reviewed periodically.

The honest trade-off: retail is still catching up to the housing. Day to day shopping mostly happens along Highway 7 and Markham Road rather than inside the neighbourhood.

## Transit and the commute

Cornell Bus Terminal on Highway 7 connects to Viva bus rapid transit running west along the corridor. Markham GO on the Stouffville line is a short drive west. Highway 407 is close, which is the main reason the neighbourhood works for people commuting east or west rather than downtown.

Scheduled weekday morning trains take 52 minutes from Markham GO to Union Station, plus the drive to the station (GO Transit timetable, September 2026).

## Who Cornell suits

It suits first time buyers, young families and anyone who wants newer housing with lower maintenance. It suits people who work at or near the hospital. It suits buyers who actively like walkable, dense street life.

It does not suit buyers who want a large private yard, mature trees or a short train ride to Union Station.
