## The short answer

Angus Glen is Markham's executive enclave. Large detached homes on wide lots, a championship golf club at the centre and a community centre and library that residents across York Region drive to use.

It is the neighbourhood people move to when the house itself is the priority rather than walkability or a train commute.

## What homes cost

From January to March 2026 TRREB recorded 22 sales in Angus Glen with a median of $1,349,000 across all property types. The table above breaks that down by property type for the same period.

Upper end pricing works differently from the mid market. The buyer pool is smaller, the comparables are thinner and a house that is priced ten percent optimistically does not simply sell slower, it can sit long enough to acquire a reputation. That is the single most important thing to understand about selling in Angus Glen.

## The golf club and the community centre

Angus Glen Golf Club is a championship facility and it gives the neighbourhood its name and much of its identity. Membership has nothing to do with home ownership, so treat the course as scenery and amenity rather than as something you are buying into.

Angus Glen Community Centre and Library anchors the community with recreation and library services and it is a legitimate draw for families.

## Parks, amenities and daily life

Green space, trail links and the golf course dominate the public realm. Shopping is a drive, mostly along Major Mackenzie Drive and Kennedy Road.

The honest trade-off: this is a car neighbourhood. There is nothing walkable, there is no main street and evenings are quiet. Buyers coming from downtown Toronto sometimes find the silence harder to adjust to than they expected.

## Transit and the commute

YRT local routes serve the main roads. Highway 404 is a short drive west. There is no GO station in the neighbourhood, so a train commute means driving to Unionville or Mount Joy first.

Scheduled weekday morning trains take 41 minutes from Unionville GO and 57 minutes from Mount Joy GO to Union Station, plus the drive to the station (GO Transit timetable, September 2026).

## Who Angus Glen suits

It suits move up buyers who need genuine space, professionals who drive rather than commute by train and downsizers who want to stay in north Markham without leaving a large home behind entirely.

It does not suit buyers who want walkability, transit access or a lower maintenance property.
