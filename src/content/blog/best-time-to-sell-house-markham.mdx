## The short answer

There is no single month that the data names as the best time to sell a house in Markham. Sales climbed through spring and early summer in both 2025 and 2026. So did new listings, which means more competition: May 2025 had 948 new listings against 248 in December 2025.

This post uses the monthly Toronto Regional Real Estate Board (TRREB) Market Watch figures for the City of Markham, all home types, from January 2025 to September 2026. If you are asking about the best time to sell a house in Ontario more broadly, the same caution applies: these are past months in one city that describe what happened rather than what will happen. A better answer for you starts with your own plans, which we cover near the end.

## When do most homes sell in Markham?

In 2025 Markham sales were highest in October (281) and June (274) and lowest in December (171). The first three months of the year were quiet, with 173 sales in January 2025 and 175 in February 2025. Activity then built through spring.

These are the monthly counts as TRREB reports them for Markham. Read them for the overall pattern across the year rather than for small gaps between neighbouring months.

| Month | Sales | New listings | Avg days on market (sold) | Avg sale price to list price | Months of inventory |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| January 2025 | 173 | 512 | 34 | 100% | 3.1 |
| February 2025 | 175 | 551 | 27 | 102% | 3.3 |
| March 2025 | 220 | 719 | 21 | 102% | 3.5 |
| April 2025 | 238 | 779 | 25 | 100% | 3.7 |
| May 2025 | 256 | 948 | 25 | 100% | 4.0 |
| June 2025 | 274 | 833 | 28 | 99% | 4.1 |
| July 2025 | 266 | 771 | 30 | 98% | 4.2 |
| August 2025 | 240 | 635 | 33 | 98% | 4.3 |
| September 2025 | 259 | 794 | 36 | 98% | 4.3 |
| October 2025 | 281 | 680 | 33 | 99% | 4.4 |
| November 2025 | 219 | 514 | 34 | 98% | 4.6 |
| December 2025 | 171 | 248 | 36 | 97% | 4.7 |

Months of inventory in this table is TRREB's 12 month moving average of active listings divided by sales, so it moves slowly and reflects the past year rather than one month.

## When do sellers face the most competition from other listings?

New listings show how many other sellers came to market in the same month. In 2025 they peaked in May at 948 and were also above 770 in April, June, July and September. They fell to 635 in August 2025 and to 248 in December 2025.

Sales and new listings do not move together month to month. October 2025 had the most sales (281) on only 680 new listings, while May 2025 had 948 new listings and 256 sales. A seller listing in May 2025 shared the market with more new listings than in any other month of that year.

## How did the first nine months of 2026 compare?

In 2026 sales were highest in June at 360, ahead of May at 303 and July at 288. January 2026 was the lowest at 163. New listings were highest in June (794), April (773) and May (753) and lowest in February at 427.

| Month | Sales | New listings | Avg days on market (sold) | Avg sale price to list price | Months of inventory |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| January 2026 | 163 | 491 | 40 | 97% | 4.7 |
| February 2026 | 182 | 427 | 40 | 98% | 4.7 |
| March 2026 | 225 | 614 | 32 | 98% | 4.7 |
| April 2026 | 250 | 773 | 27 | 98% | 4.6 |
| May 2026 | 303 | 753 | 27 | 99% | 4.5 |
| June 2026 | 360 | 794 | 29 | 99% | 4.3 |
| July 2026 | 288 | 704 | 32 | 99% | 4.2 |
| August 2026 | 282 | 584 | 33 | 98% | 4.1 |
| September 2026 | 245 | 729 | 34 | 98% | 4.1 |

The pattern matches 2025 in outline: fewer sales and listings in the winter months, then a build into late spring and early summer. The 2026 rows end in September, so the rest of the year is not yet known. For the latest month in detail, see our [September 2026 market report](/market-reports/september-2026/).

## How long do homes take to sell in Markham and at what price?

Average days on market for sold listings was shortest in March 2025 at 21. In 2026 it was shortest in April and May at 27 each. It was longest in January and February 2026 at 40 each. Homes sold in September 2025 and December 2025 took 36 days on average.

The average sale price to list price ratio was 102% in February and March 2025. From December 2025 through September 2026 it stayed between 97% and 99%. Months of inventory rose from 3.1 in January 2025 to 4.7 from December 2025 through March 2026 and was 4.1 in September 2026.

One caution about prices. Average and median prices move with the mix of homes that sold in a month. The median price was $1,260,000 in June 2025 and $1,094,000 in August 2025. In 2026 it was $1,052,500 in February and $1,160,000 in May but $1,071,000 in June. A change like that can come from a different mix of homes selling in one month rather than from any one home being worth more or less. For that reason we do not treat these figures as proof that prices rise in spring. For price levels over time, see our [Markham house prices](/markham-house-prices/) page.

## Which timing factors depend on you as a seller?

The monthly data cannot see your situation. These are the factors that sit with the seller:

- **Your next home.** If you are buying as well as selling, your purchase dates matter more than the month. Our post on [selling first or buying first](/blog/sell-first-or-buy-first-downsizing-markham/) explains both paths.
- **Your closing date flexibility.** A sale that needs to close on a fixed day has fewer options than one with room to move.
- **Your school year.** Some families prefer to move between school years. School boundaries are set by the school board and must be confirmed for the exact address you are moving to.
- **How long preparation takes.** Repairs and cleaning take time. Our [selling timeline](/blog/selling-a-house-in-markham-timeline/) lays out the steps from preparation to closing.
- **Your reason for moving.** Sellers who are [upsizing](/upsizing-markham/) or [downsizing](/downsizing-markham/) often have a date they are working toward.

Talk to your lawyer, accountant and mortgage professional about how timing affects your own finances.
