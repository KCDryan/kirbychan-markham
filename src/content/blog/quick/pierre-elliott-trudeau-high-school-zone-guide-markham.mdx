## The short answer

* Pierre Elliott Trudeau High School serves an attendance area set by the York Region District School Board (YRDSB), not by real estate marketing boundaries.
* Boundaries are confirmed by address, not by neighbourhood name and the board reviews them annually.
* No address guarantees a place. The board says some new students may be placed in a different school because of growth.
* French Immersion at the school is offered to students within its boundary who meet the minimum French hours.
* Confirm the exact address with the board before writing an offer, not after closing.

## Where is the Pierre Elliott Trudeau High School zone in Markham?

Pierre Elliott Trudeau High School is part of the YRDSB system. The board sets its attendance area and I cannot tell you from a map or a neighbourhood name whether a given home is inside it. The board publishes the boundary for each school and the [Berczy Village](/berczy-village-markham/) and [Wismer](/wismer-markham/) guides on this site mention the school only as one of the secondary options linked to those areas.

This matters more than it sounds like it should. A listing that says "in a great school area" is a general impression, not a confirmed fact. The board's own locator is the source to rely on and even it carries a caution that new students may sometimes be placed elsewhere.

## How to confirm your home is in the Pierre Elliott Trudeau High School zone

Before you fall in love with a property because of its assigned high school, take a few minutes to check it properly. This is the order I suggest to clients:

* Get the exact civic address of the property.
* Enter the street number and street name in the [YRDSB School Locator](https://schoollocator.yrdsb.ca/). It only recognizes Region of York addresses.
* Look at the elementary and secondary schools it lists, since families often care about both. A PDF boundary map is available with the results.
* Contact the school or the board to verify, especially if you are buying a new home in a growing community. The board advises new residents to check with the local school.
* Ask me or your lawyer how to handle this in an offer if the school is important to your purchase.

## What programs does Pierre Elliott Trudeau High School offer?

The school's programs page lists French Immersion, a Specialist High Skills Major under its pathways programs and clubs and athletics. Programs change over time, so treat this list and any list you read online, as a starting point. If a specific program is the reason you are considering a home, check the school's [programs page](https://trudeau-hs.yrdsb.ca/our-programs) first.

### French Immersion and boundary

According to the school, French Immersion is offered to students within the Pierre Elliott Trudeau boundary who have the minimum required hours of French language. That means the boundary is only one of the conditions. Confirm the current requirements with the school.

## What happens if school boundaries change after you buy?

The board says attendance areas are reviewed annually under Board Policy 108 and that portable classrooms are used during peak enrolment or while new schools and additions are built. Growth can also mean new students are placed temporarily in another school. So the boundary that applies on the day you buy may not be the one that applies later.

If you are buying in a newer part of Markham, ask the board whether any review affects the address. For how placement works for current students and siblings, ask the board directly.
