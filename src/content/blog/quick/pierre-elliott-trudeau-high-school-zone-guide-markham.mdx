## The short answer

* Pierre Elliott Trudeau High School serves a specific catchment area within Markham defined by YRDSB, not by real estate marketing boundaries.
* Boundaries are checked by address, not by neighbourhood name and can change between school years.
* Homes confirmed in the zone often carry a price premium, but the size of that premium varies by street and property type.
* Being in the boundary guarantees enrolment eligibility, but specialty programs may have separate admission rules.
* Buyers should verify catchment status directly with YRDSB before writing an offer, not after closing.

## Where is the Pierre Elliott Trudeau High School zone in Markham?

Pierre Elliott Trudeau High School sits within the York Region District School Board system and its catchment draws from a cluster of Markham neighbourhoods rather than one single subdivision. Like most secondary school boundaries in York Region, the exact lines are drawn street by street and are adjusted periodically as new housing gets built and enrolment shifts.

This matters more than it sounds like it should. Two homes that look identical, on the same street, a few doors apart, can sometimes fall on opposite sides of a boundary line. I have seen buyers assume a home was in a school zone because a neighbour's kid attended that school, only to find out later that the boundary had shifted or that the neighbour's home was simply on the correct side of a line the buyer's home was not.

### Why boundaries are not the same as neighbourhood names

Markham's neighbourhoods have informal names used in real estate listings and community groups, but YRDSB boundaries are drawn independently of those names. A listing that says "in a great school area" is describing a general impression, not a confirmed fact. The board's own locator tool, searchable by address, is the only source that should be treated as final.

## How to confirm your home is in the Pierre Elliott Trudeau High School zone

Before you fall in love with a property because of its assigned high school, take five minutes to verify it properly. Here is the order I recommend to clients:

* Get the exact civic address of the property, including unit number if it applies.
* Enter that address into the YRDSB school locator on their official website.
* Confirm both the elementary and secondary school assignments, since families often care about both.
* Call the school board directly if the online tool gives an ambiguous result or if you are buying a pre-construction home in a newer area.
* Ask your realtor to include a boundary confirmation clause in any offer where school zone is a stated condition of purchase.

Skipping this step is one of the more common regrets I hear about after closing. A buyer assumes catchment based on a map they saw online and finds out months later that the actual boundary line runs down the middle of their street.

## What programs does Pierre Elliott Trudeau High School offer?

Secondary schools across York Region typically offer the standard Ontario curriculum plus a mix of enriched options. For a school like Pierre Elliott Trudeau, this usually includes some combination of French Immersion or Extended French, Advanced Placement or enriched academic streams, co-operative education placements and a range of athletics, arts and club programming.

Program availability changes from year to year depending on enrolment and staffing, so treat any list you read online, including this one, as a starting point rather than a guarantee. If a specific program is the reason you are considering a home in this zone, call the school directly or check the current course calendar published by YRDSB before you make a decision based on it.

### Specialty programs and separate admission

Being inside the Pierre Elliott Trudeau High School zone means your child is entitled to attend the school for regular programming. It does not automatically guarantee a spot in every specialty stream. Programs like French Immersion, IB-style enriched tracks or specific arts and athletics academies sometimes have their own application windows, capacity limits or even board-wide lottery systems that operate separately from catchment residency. Confirm this distinction early if a specific program, not just the school itself, is driving your home search.

## Do homes in the Pierre Elliott Trudeau catchment cost more?

In general, confirmed access to a well-regarded school does add a premium to home prices and Markham buyers are not shy about asking for school zone confirmation before they even book a showing. That said, the premium is rarely a flat percentage across an entire catchment. It tends to concentrate on specific streets, specific home sizes and specific school pairings (elementary plus secondary combinations that families particularly want).

Price also depends heavily on lot size, age of the home, proximity to transit and shopping and general condition. A smaller or older home inside the zone can still sell for less than a larger, updated home just outside it. School zone is one factor among several, not the only one driving value.

| Factor                             | Typically increases value                   | Typically decreases value                           |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Confirmed high school catchment    | Well-regarded, in-demand school             | School slated for boundary review or overcrowding   |

| Proximity to transit and amenities | Close to GO stations, major roads, shopping | Isolated from transit or services                   |
| School program fit                 | Access to specialty program family wants    | Program unavailable or waitlisted despite catchment |

## What happens if school boundaries change after you buy?

YRDSB, like most growing school boards in the GTA, reviews boundaries periodically. This happens most often in areas with new residential development, where a wave of new families can push an existing school past capacity and force a redrawing of catchment lines. If you are buying in a newer part of Markham or near one, it is worth asking whether any boundary review is planned or underway for the Pierre Elliott Trudeau High School zone specifically.

A boundary review does not usually force a currently enrolled student to switch schools mid-program in most cases, boards often grandfather existing students through to graduation, but incoming students to a household (a younger sibling, for example) may be assigned differently. If you have multiple children at different ages, this is worth understanding clearly before you buy.
