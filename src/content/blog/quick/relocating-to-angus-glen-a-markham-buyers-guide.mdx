## The short answer

* Angus Glen is a Markham neighbourhood built mostly from the late 1990s onward, so homes tend to be newer with larger lots than older parts of the city.
* It is served by the York Region District School Board and York Catholic District School Board and school registration should be started as soon as you have an address.
* Commuting to Toronto usually takes 40 to 60 minutes by car, with GO Transit and York Region Transit as alternatives worth checking against your actual work location.
* Housing is mostly detached and larger townhomes, with fewer condo options than closer to downtown Markham or along major transit corridors.
* If you are selling a home elsewhere to fund the move, line up financing and a realistic closing timeline before you make an offer here.

## What Angus Glen Actually Is

Angus Glen is a planned residential community in Markham, Ontario, built around two well-known golf clubs and developed largely from the late 1990s through the 2000s. It is not a downtown or high-rise neighbourhood. It reads more like a mature suburban community with curving streets, larger lots and a mix of housing types aimed at families and move-up buyers.

People relocating to Angus Glen from Toronto or from outside the GTA are usually drawn by the same things: newer construction compared to older Markham neighbourhoods, good schools and a quieter, more residential feel while still being within reach of Highway 404 and major transit lines. It is a strong fit if your priorities are space and schools. It is a weaker fit if you want walkable urban amenities or the lowest entry price in the region.

## Housing Options When Relocating To Angus Glen

The housing stock in Angus Glen leans heavily toward detached homes, many built with larger family layouts, along with a good supply of freehold and some condo townhomes. There are pockets of lower-rise condo buildings for buyers who want less exterior maintenance, but this is not a neighbourhood built around high-rise living.

Prices vary by street, lot size, age of the home and whether it backs onto the golf course or a ravine. Rather than quote a specific figure here, which can be out of date within months, ask for a current comparative market analysis on the specific style and size of home you want. That gives you a realistic range based on recent closed sales, not asking prices.

### What Buyers Typically Compare

* Detached homes on larger lots, generally the highest price point in the neighbourhood.
* Freehold townhomes, a common middle ground for families who want a house but a smaller footprint.
* Condo townhomes and low-rise condo units, for buyers prioritizing lower maintenance.

## Schools And Family Considerations

Angus Glen falls under the York Region District School Board for public schools and the York Catholic District School Board for Catholic schools. There are also private school options in the broader Markham area that some relocating families consider.

If schooling is a major factor in your move, confirm the exact school your target address is zoned for directly with the relevant board before you finalize a purchase. Boundaries do shift over time and an address a few streets over can fall into a different school zone than you expect.

Families relocating to Angus Glen with school-aged children should also start registration paperwork as early as possible. Requirements typically include proof of address, proof of age and immunization records and processing takes time, especially mid-school-year.

## Commuting And Getting Around

Angus Glen sits roughly 30 to 40 kilometres north of downtown Toronto. Driving time to downtown is generally 40 to 60 minutes outside of rush hour and noticeably longer during peak commute windows on Highway 404 or the Don Valley Parkway.

For those who prefer not to drive the whole distance, GO Transit rail and bus services connect Markham to Toronto and York Region Transit (YRT) covers local routes within Markham. Many residents combine a short drive or bus ride to a GO station with a train into the city. If your job has a fixed location, it is worth doing a test commute at the actual time of day you would be travelling before committing to a purchase.

## Planning A Move To Angus Glen From Out Of Town

If you are relocating to Angus Glen from another city or province, the biggest planning risk is timeline mismatch: your job start date, your current home's sale and your new home's closing date rarely line up perfectly on the first try. Build in buffer time wherever you can and talk to your lender early about pre-approval so financing is not the thing holding up your closing date.

If your employer is offering a relocation package, ask specifically what it covers: temporary housing, moving costs, closing cost reimbursement or a lump sum you manage yourself. Get those details in writing before you make decisions based on assumptions about what will be reimbursed.

### A Realistic Relocation Checklist

* Get pre-approved for financing before you start touring homes seriously.
* Confirm school zoning for any address you are seriously considering.
* Test the commute at your actual working hours, not off-peak.
* Line up a local lawyer for closing and a home inspector if the property is not new.
* Budget for land transfer tax, legal fees and moving costs separately from your down payment.

## Costs To Budget Beyond The Purchase Price

Relocating anywhere in Ontario involves costs beyond the sale price of the home itself. Land transfer tax applies at the provincial level and Toronto has an additional municipal land transfer tax that does not apply in Markham, which is worth noting if you are comparing costs against a City of Toronto purchase. Rates and any first-time buyer rebates change periodically, so confirm the current figures with your lawyer or the Ontario Ministry of Finance before closing.

Other costs to plan for include legal fees, a home inspection if you are buying a resale property, moving company fees and possibly a period of overlap if your old and new homes do not close on the same day. None of these are unique to Angus Glen, but they matter more when you are also managing a long-distance move.
