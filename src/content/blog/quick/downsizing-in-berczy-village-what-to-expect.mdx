Downsizing out of Berczy Village is usually a two neighbourhood decision. The sale happens here. The purchase almost always happens somewhere else in Markham.

## Why the sale is the easier half

Berczy Village is north Markham family territory: detached homes from the late 1990s onward, freehold townhomes and semis, quiet crescents and a school reputation that does a lot of the selling. Buyers arrive already sold on the area. Our [Berczy Village neighbourhood guide](/berczy-village-markham/) covers the housing stock and the transit picture in detail.

What that means in practice is simple. You are selling into steady family demand and the work is presentation, timing and pricing rather than convincing anyone the neighbourhood is worth moving to.

## Why the purchase takes longer

There is very little condo stock in Berczy Village. If your plan is a smaller freehold townhome or a semi, you may be able to stay. If your plan is an apartment with no exterior maintenance, you are looking at [Unionville](/unionville-markham/), [Downtown Markham](/downtown-markham/) or Thornhill and that changes the search, the price and the timeline.

This is the part people underestimate. Deciding where you are going is what makes everything else possible to plan. Until the destination is settled, you cannot judge how much time you need between the two closings, whether bridge financing is worth discussing with your lender or how firm your listing date should be.

## A sensible order of work

1. Settle the destination first: property type, then neighbourhood, then building or street.
2. Get an honest read on what your Berczy home is worth today, so the numbers are real before you plan around them.
3. Map the two closings and decide how much overlap you need.
4. Start sorting the contents early. In a home held for twenty years or more, this is the step that sets the pace of the whole move.
5. Prepare the house for the buyers it will actually attract, which here means families.
