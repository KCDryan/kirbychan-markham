## Why homeowners are downsizing in Milliken Mills

Milliken Mills, split into the East and West pockets of Markham near Kennedy Road and Steeles Avenue, is one of those neighbourhoods where a lot of original buyers purchased detached and semi-detached homes decades ago and simply stayed. Many of those houses are now four-bedroom family homes with one or two people living in them. The stairs, the yard and the square footage that made sense when kids were at home stop making sense once they move out.

For a lot of these households, the home itself has become the largest asset they own by a wide margin. Years of appreciation in Milliken Mills East and West mean that selling and moving into something smaller is not just about lifestyle, it is a real financial decision with meaningful numbers attached. That is exactly why downsizing in Milliken Mills deserves a proper plan rather than a quick decision made after one bad winter of shovelling snow.

## Selling first or buying first: the order that works for you

One of the first practical questions in any downsizing plan is sequencing. Do you sell your current home and then look for the next one or do you buy first and carry two properties for a while?

*   **Sell first:** You know exactly how much you have to work with before committing to a new purchase, which removes a lot of financial guesswork. The tradeoff is you may need a longer closing, a rent-back arrangement or a short-term rental between homes.
*   **Buy first:** You avoid moving twice and can take your time finding the right fit, but you need financing or savings to cover both properties until the first one sells and that can mean a bridge loan or line of credit.

How long a home takes to sell depends on price point, condition, the season and how a specific property is priced. Your agent can estimate a realistic timeline based on recent comparable sales and that timeline should factor into whichever sequence you choose.

## Where Milliken Mills downsizers tend to move

Some homeowners want to stay in the neighbourhood they know, close to the same grocery stores, bus stops and neighbours. Others are ready for a change of pace entirely. Both are common among people downsizing in Milliken Mills.

*   A condo in Milliken Mills East or West, often along Kennedy Road or near Steeles Avenue, keeps you close to transit and familiar amenities.
*   A bungalow or smaller detached home elsewhere in Markham, such as Unionville or Raymerville, trades square footage for single-level living while keeping a small yard.
*   A condo or townhome further from Markham, sometimes closer to adult children or in a lower-cost part of the GTA, can stretch the proceeds from a Milliken Mills sale significantly further.

## What downsizing actually costs

The sale price of your current home and the purchase price of the next one are only part of the picture. Ontario buyers pay provincial land transfer tax on any purchase, calculated on a sliding scale based on the purchase price and buyers within the City of Toronto pay an additional municipal land transfer tax on top of that. Markham falls under the provincial tax only, not the Toronto municipal tax, but the exact rate brackets should always be confirmed with your lawyer or the Ontario Ministry of Finance since they are updated periodically.

Beyond land transfer tax, budget for legal fees, title insurance, a home inspection on the new property if applicable, moving costs and if you are buying a condo, ongoing condo fees that a detached home never had. On the selling side, real estate commission, staging and any repairs needed to get the home market-ready also come out of your proceeds. None of these numbers are fixed, so ask for written estimates specific to your situation rather than relying on a rule of thumb.

## Decluttering and managing the move

For a lot of long-term Milliken Mills homeowners, the hardest part of downsizing is not the real estate, it is the decades of belongings that come with raising a family in one house. Furniture, keepsakes and a basement full of things nobody has touched in years all need a decision.

Starting early makes a real difference. Going room by room, months ahead of listing and sorting into keep, sell, donate and discard categories turns an overwhelming task into a manageable one. For seniors or anyone who finds the process too much to handle alone, professional move management and downsizing services operate across the Greater Toronto Area and can handle everything from sorting to the physical move itself. The National Association of Specialty & Senior Move Managers maintains a directory of accredited providers serving Markham and the surrounding region if that kind of support would help.
