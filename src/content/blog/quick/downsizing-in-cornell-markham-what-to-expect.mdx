Downsizing in Cornell does not have to mean leaving and that changes how the whole move is planned.

## The neighbourhood does the work

Cornell is a planned community in east Markham built on a grid, with front porches, rear lane garages and Markham Stouffville Hospital close by, which [Oak Valley Health lists](https://www.oakvalleyhealth.ca/) at 381 Church Street. The housing mix is the point: freehold townhomes, semis and detached homes on the grid, plus newer condo stock. Our [Cornell neighbourhood guide](/cornell-markham/) sets out the layout, the housing types and the transit.

For a downsizer that mix can be an advantage. Moving from a house to an apartment often means changing neighbourhood as well as home. In Cornell you may be able to stay inside the same community and keep your doctors and your routines.

## What still needs planning

The sale and the purchase are still two transactions that have to fit together.

- **Timing.** Two closings on the same day sounds tidy and leaves no room for anything to slip. Decide early how much overlap you want and ask a mortgage professional about bridge financing before you need it.
- **Condo fees and rules.** If you are moving into a condo townhome or an apartment, read what the fees cover and what the rules say about pets, parking and visitors. The [Condo Authority of Ontario](https://www.condoauthorityontario.ca/condo-living/corporate-records/status-certificates/) explains that the status certificate covers the budget, reserve fund information, special assessments, the bylaws and rules and any litigation, so have your lawyer review it.
- **Contents.** Decades in a family home take longer to sort than anyone expects. Start before you list, room by room.
- **Presentation.** Buyers are likely to compare your home with others on the same grid, so condition and staging matter. See what is [for sale nearby](/homes-for-sale/) to understand the competition.
