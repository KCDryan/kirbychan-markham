## The short answer

* Unionville is a historic village-turned-neighbourhood inside the City of [Markham](/neighbourhoods/) and Main Street Unionville is a designated heritage area.
* Main Street has heritage buildings, restaurants and shops and the Unionville Village Festival is held there each June.
* Housing stock ranges from older homes near Main Street to newer townhomes and detached houses toward the edges, with prices reflecting that mix.
* Toogood Pond is a City of Markham park on Carlton Road.
* Commuters can use Unionville GO on the Stouffville line for trips to Union Station.

## What makes Unionville different from the rest of Markham

Markham is a large city with several distinct pockets and Unionville is the one most people picture when they hear the name. It grew from a 19th-century village and the City of Markham lists Unionville among its heritage conservation districts. That designation is a big part of why the neighbourhood looks the way it does today.

Walk along Main Street and you will see heritage buildings now housing cafes, restaurants and small shops, close to Toogood Pond and mature trees. It reads more like a small Ontario town centre than a suburb and that contrast is a big part of the appeal for buyers who want character in their surroundings.

Outside the historic core, Unionville extends into more conventional residential streets: detached houses from different building eras, some townhome clusters and pockets of newer construction. When people talk about living in Unionville, they are usually talking about two slightly different experiences depending on which street they end up on. The full [Unionville neighbourhood guide](/unionville-markham/) has current TRREB price bands by property type.

## Schools and family life in Unionville

Unionville is served by the York Region District School Board, along with the Catholic board and other options. I do not rank schools and I cannot tell you that an address sits in a particular school's boundary.

School boundaries are set by the board and can change. The YRDSB runs a school locator where you enter a street number and name to see the designated school. Confirm the exact school for the specific address, not just the general neighbourhood, directly with the board before you write an offer.

## Getting around: commuting from Unionville

Unionville GO is on the Stouffville line. On the GO Transit service change notice, a morning rush hour trip departs Unionville GO at 7:59 a.m. and arrives at Union Station at 8:40 a.m. That makes downtown Toronto commuting realistic without a car for anyone working near the core. Timetables change, so check the current schedule on gotransit.com.

Metrolinx completed upgrades at the station in 2022, including new elevators, pedestrian tunnels, a second track and 286 net new parking spots.

If you drive, test the commute at your actual commute times before assuming a map estimate holds up. Within the neighbourhood, many errands are walkable if you live close to Main Street. Further out, you will likely want a car for groceries and big-box shopping.

## Parks and things to do in Unionville

Toogood Pond is a City of Markham park at 218 Carlton Road with washrooms and Toogood Park, at 58 Scriberras Road, has a playground and tennis court. Both are popular for walks.

Main Street itself functions as a destination, not just a through street. The City of Markham lists the Unionville Village Festival in June, a Heritage and Big Band Festival in September and a Simcoe Day Festival in August among the heritage celebrations on Main Street Unionville. The Markham Museum is another part of the city heritage offering.

## Housing in Unionville

Housing in Unionville is more varied than its reputation suggests. Near the historic core you will find older detached homes on established lots with mature trees. Moving outward, the housing mix shifts toward more recent houses and newer townhome developments.

Detached homes generally price above many other Markham areas, though the gap depends heavily on the street and the era of the home.
