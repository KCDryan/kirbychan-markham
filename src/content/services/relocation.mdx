## What we do differently

**We tell you what you cannot afford early.** The most common relocation failure is spending six weeks looking in a neighbourhood that was never within budget. That conversation is uncomfortable once and useful forever.

**We check the commute at the time you will actually make it.** A drive that takes twenty minutes on a Sunday afternoon is a different drive at eight on a Tuesday. We will tell you to test it yourself and we will tell you when the train is the only sane option.

**We are specific about trade-offs.** Every Markham neighbourhood gives you something and takes something away. Our neighbourhood guides are written to say both parts out loud and the conversation works the same way.

## Where people relocate from

Downtown Toronto, elsewhere in the GTA, other Canadian cities and overseas. Each brings a different set of assumptions and the overseas conversation in particular needs more time on things that locals take for granted: winter, school registration, credit history and how conditional offers work here.
