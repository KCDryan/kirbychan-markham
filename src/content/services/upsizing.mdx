## What we do differently

**We start with the lender, not with listings.** An upsizing plan built without knowing what you can carry is not a plan. One conversation with your lender at the beginning prevents the two worst outcomes in this move.

**We say when a condition will not fly.** A condition on the sale of your home protects you and in a competitive offer it will usually lose you the house. You deserve to know which situation you are in before you write the offer, not after you lose it.

**We keep both transactions in one head.** When the sale and the purchase are handled by two people who do not talk to each other, the closing dates are where it shows. We run both sides so the dates are built together from the start.

## The common Markham path

Out of a condo or townhome in Downtown Markham, Milliken Mills or Greensborough and into a detached house in Wismer, Berczy Village or Cornell. That route runs often enough that we can tell you fairly precisely what the gap looks like at current prices once we know your numbers.
