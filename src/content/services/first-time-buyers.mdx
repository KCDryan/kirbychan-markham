## What we do differently

**We work from the payment, not the pre-approval.** Lenders tell you the maximum they will lend. That is a useful fact and a terrible plan. We start from what you can pay every month without resenting it.

**We name the trade-off out loud.** In Markham, a first purchase is usually a choice between space, location and property type. You get two. Deciding which two, deliberately and early, is most of the work.

**We do not rush you into an offer.** If a property is wrong, it is wrong. There will be another. The pressure to act immediately is the single most expensive force acting on a first-time buyer and part of our job is to absorb it rather than pass it on.

## Where first-time buyers usually land in Markham

Milliken Mills for the lowest entry point and the best transit. Greensborough and Cornell for newer attached housing. Downtown Markham for condo living with everything walkable. Box Grove if the commute runs east or west on Highway 407.
