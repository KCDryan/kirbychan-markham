## What we do differently

**We work to your pace, not to a listing calendar.** Most downsizing moves fail on timing rather than on price. If you need four months to be ready, we plan for four months.

**We do not tell you to renovate a house you are leaving.** The spending that returns money before a sale is a short list. We will hand you that list and tell you to ignore the rest.

**We treat the emotional part as real.** Leaving a house you have been in since your children were small is not a transaction. It is fine for it to take the time it takes and we would rather you felt settled about the decision than fast.

## Where this comes up in Markham

Markham Village, the Markham side of Thornhill and the older parts of Unionville and Milliken Mills, where families have often been in the same house for thirty or forty years. The usual destinations are condos in Downtown Markham, bungalows and townhomes across the city or a move closer to where adult children now live.
