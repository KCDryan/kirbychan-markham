## What we do differently

**We work at the estate's pace.** There is a legal process with its own timeline and a family with its own. We fit around both rather than pushing a listing date.

**We put things in writing.** An executor may have to explain decisions to beneficiaries, sometimes years later. Written opinions of value, written recommendations and a record of the offers received make that straightforward.

**We are calm about the contents.** Almost every estate sale stalls at the same place: nobody wants to be the person who decides what happens to a parent's belongings. We have seen it many times, we will not rush you and we can bring in people who do this work with care.

## Where this comes up in Markham

Most often in Markham Village, the Markham side of Thornhill, the older parts of Unionville and Milliken Mills, where original owners have frequently been in the same house since the 1960s or 1970s.

## Please get proper advice

Estate administration involves legal and tax questions that are outside what a real estate brokerage can advise on. Engage an estate lawyer and an accountant. We will coordinate with them and handle the property.
