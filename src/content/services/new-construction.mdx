  - q: Do I need my own agent to buy from a builder?

    a: In the usual arrangement the builder handles the co-operating brokerage compensation. Confirm the specifics for the project in question rather than assuming.

## What we do differently

**We assume the contract is the product.** With a resale house you are buying a building you can stand in. With pre-construction you are buying a document. We treat it that way and we push you toward a lawyer who reads these agreements for a living.

**We put the closing costs on paper up front.** The gap between the purchase price and the money you actually need on closing day is where most of the unhappy surprises in new construction live.

**We will tell you when resale is the better buy.** Frequently it is. A three year old house with the landscaping finished, the window coverings in and no closing adjustments left to pay is often a better value than the same house bought from a plan.

Condo and townhome projects in and around Downtown Markham and the Highway 7 corridor, plus continuing phases of newer development across the north and east of the city.
