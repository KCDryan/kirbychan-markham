## What we do differently

**We underwrite on your assumptions and ours.** You will see both, side by side. If our vacancy and maintenance numbers are more conservative than yours, you will know exactly where the disagreement is and you can decide who is right.

**We will talk you out of a deal.** It happens regularly. A property that looks fine at a glance falls apart once the fees, the taxes and a realistic repair allowance are in the model. That conversation is the service.

**We do not forecast prices.** Nobody can tell you what Markham will be worth in five years. Anyone who does is selling you something. We work on what a property earns and costs today and treat appreciation as an unpriced possibility rather than a plan.

## An honest caution

Rental property is a business with legal obligations, real costs and real risk. It is not passive income and the rules governing residential tenancies in Ontario are substantial. Go in with professional advice from people qualified to give it.
