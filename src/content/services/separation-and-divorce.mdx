## How we approach it

**Equal information for both owners.** Every update, showing report and offer goes to both owners at the same time. Nobody is left wondering what the other person was told.

**Calm and practical.** We keep conversations focused on the decisions the sale needs, not on anything else. If it helps, we communicate with each owner separately.

**Working alongside your lawyers.** Your lawyers handle the legal questions. We keep them informed about the sale so closing goes smoothly.

## A note on timing

Some owners need to sell quickly. Others need time before a sale makes sense. We will explain what the Markham market is doing and what that means for timing, then leave the decision with you and your advisors.
